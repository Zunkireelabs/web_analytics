Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about ordering Trailhead Gear Co. products.

Orders & Shipping

How long will my order take to arrive?

Most U.S. orders ship within 2 business days and arrive in 3–7 business days depending on your location. You’ll receive tracking information as soon as your order leaves our warehouse.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship to many countries worldwide. Shipping times and rates are calculated at checkout based on your destination and order size.

Products & Care

How do I care for my backpack or tent?

Spot clean with mild soap and water, let it fully air-dry, and store in a cool, dry place. Avoid machine washing, dryers, and harsh detergents to preserve the technical fabrics.

What is your return and warranty policy?

If your gear isn’t working for you, you can return unused items within 30 days. Manufacturing defects are covered by a limited lifetime warranty—contact our support team with photos and your order number.

How do I choose the right pack size?

Use our size guide on each product page to match your torso length and typical trip length. If you’re between sizes, we recommend sizing up for multi-day trips.
