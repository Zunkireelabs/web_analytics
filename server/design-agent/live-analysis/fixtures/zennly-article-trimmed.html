Salon enquiry management in Nepal: turn phone, Instagram and WhatsApp enquiries into bookings

Most salon enquiries are lost while the stylist is busy with a client. One booking link lets the client book without waiting for a reply.

Salon enquiry management means answering every phone call, Instagram message and WhatsApp question quickly and turning it into a booked appointment. The simplest way is to give every enquirer one online booking link, so they book a stylist and a time without waiting for a reply.

Where salon enquiries get lost

A parlour hears from clients in four places at once: the phone, Instagram, WhatsApp and the front desk. Each message asks the same thing: are you free, how much is it, can I come at five? When the stylist is busy with a client, the message waits, and the person books somewhere else.

  • Slow replies while staff are with clients.
  • Enquiries spread across apps, so nobody knows who was already answered.

A simple way to manage enquiries

  1. Pick one place to answer. Choose who watches the phone, Instagram and WhatsApp, and when.
  2. Reply with a link, not a back-and-forth. Send the same online booking link every time, so the client chooses a service, a stylist and a free slot.

What to do on a busy day

When a day is full, a waiting list keeps the people who could not get a slot, and a short message the next day brings some of them back to book another time with you.

How Zennly fits

With online booking, an enquiry becomes a booking without a reply: the client picks the service, the stylist and a time, and the slot is held. Each booking checks the staff member and time, so there are no double-bookings for the people on your team.
